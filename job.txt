What is the Opportunity?

Safer. Faster. Smarter. This is how Aecon Utilities is committed to delivering projects. Aecon Utilities is a leading North American utility infrastructure provider with a strong track record delivering safe, reliable and innovative solutions across electrical, telecommunications, pipeline distribution and renewable energy markets. With operations across North America, Aecon Utilities supports critical utility infrastructure that powers and keeps communities connected. Guided by our core value of Safety Always, we partner with clients to build the infrastructure that powers the future.

The Estimating team at Aecon plays a critical role in the successful delivery of projects by developing accurate, comprehensive cost estimates and supporting the tendering process. They analyze project documentation, collaborate with operations, and engage with clients, suppliers, and subcontractors to ensure competitive and complete submissions. Beyond bid stage, the team supports project execution by tracking financial performance, managing scope changes, and driving continuous improvement through post-project analysis.

Under the supervision of a Proposal Supervisor or Proposal Lead, the Proposals Coordinator Co-op supports the proposal team in the coordination and development of RFx (Request for Proposal, Request for Quotation, etc.) submissions.
This role is designed for a college or university student looking to gain hands-on experience in proposal coordination, business development, and construction/utility services. The co-op will assist with administrative, formatting, and coordination tasks while learning proposal fundamentals, tools, and processes in a fast-paced team environment.
Co-op opportunities vary in length, this opportunity currently has openings for a 12-month work term starting in January 2027.
What You'll Do Here:
Support the proposal team on active RFx submissions by assisting with coordination, document preparation, and administrative tasks.
Review RFx documents to help identify basic submission requirements, forms, and deadlines (with guidance).
Assist in maintaining proposal schedules, trackers, and document libraries.
Prepare and format proposal documents using templates, branding standards, and style guides.
Support document control and version management in SharePoint and internal systems.
Assist in assembling proposal submissions, including compiling content, formatting, and packaging deliverables.
Take notes during proposal meetings (kickoffs, reviews) and help track action items.
Conduct basic quality checks for formatting consistency and completeness.
Support updates to content libraries, including resumes, project profiles, and past submissions.
Participate in team meetings, training sessions, and continuous improvement activities.
What You Bring to the Team:
Currently enrolled in a college or university program in Communications, Marketing, Business, Engineering, or a related field.
Strong attention to detail and willingness to work within structured processes.
Strong written and verbal communication skills.
Basic proficiency in Microsoft Office (Word, PowerPoint, Excel).
Comfortable working in a team environment and collaborating with multiple stakeholders.
Strong organizational skills and ability to manage multiple tasks with guidance.
Interest in learning proposal development, business development, or the utilities/construction industry.
What You'll Gain
Exposure to the full proposal lifecycle (bid/no-bid through submission).
Hands-on experience working with real client submissions and deadlines.
Training on proposal tools, systems, and templates used in a large organization.
Understanding of how estimating, operations, and business development teams collaborate.
Opportunity to build foundational skills for a career in proposals, coordination, or business development.
